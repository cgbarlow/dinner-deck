# Dinner Deck

An interactive weekly meal planner over the recipes and groceries already held in Iris. Deal recipe cards onto a week and the shopping list writes itself, grouped by supermarket aisle.

Status: concept and mockup only. Nothing is built.

## The idea in three lines

1. 43 recipes are a deck of cards. Drag seven onto a week, or press "Deal me a week".
2. The shopping list rebuilds as you go, in aisle order, with quantities added up across meals.
3. It spots things the list alone hides: an ingredient two meals share, and a cook-once-eat-twice chain.
